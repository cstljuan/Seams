To determine the resources needed for its system, FPL has expanded and modernized its resource adequacy planning to reflect the evolving characteristics of its system and the conditions under which reliability risks can occur. Historically, FPL relied on traditional deterministic and probabilistic reliability criteria focused on peak period conditions. As FPL’s generation and load portfolio has evolved, these peak- based metrics alone are no longer sufficient to identify periods of greatest reliability risk. Accordingly, FPL enhanced its planning framework by applying a comprehensive, stochastic loss of load probability (LOLP) analysis in 2025 that evaluates system reliability across every hour of the year. This approach captures variability in customer demand, generating unit availability, and solar performance, identifies hours when reserves are most constrained-including periods of peak net demand-and informs the selection of resources capable of addressing those needs. FPL’s proposed resource additions through 2029 reflect the results of this enhanced analysis, and projected additions in the 2030 through 2035 timeframe will continue to be evaluated using these comprehensive methods to ensure resource adequacy for customers under a wide range of system conditions. Regarding FPL’s fuel mix, FPL delivered approximately 20% of its energy from nuclear generation and 11% from solar generation during 2025. Nearly all the remainder of FPL’s energy generation in 2025 came from natural gas. By 2035, the last year of the ten-year reporting period addressed in this document, the percentage of the total energy delivered to all customers on FPL’s system is projected to be approximately 16% from nuclear and 26% from solar generation. New cost-effective solar will provide fuel diversity and energy independence by reducing the amount of natural gas FPL will use to generate electricity and adding battery storage will provide cost-effective capacity to help maintain system resource adequacy. This diversity will also help to act as a hedge against swings in natural gas price volatility, providing additional savings to FPL customers during these periods.
