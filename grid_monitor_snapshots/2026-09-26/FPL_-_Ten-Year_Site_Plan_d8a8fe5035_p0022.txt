Summary of Projected Changes in Resources: A summary of the projected resources, including additions and retirements, is presented below. This discussion is presented in terms of the various types of resource options (such as solar and battery storage) in the resource plan. Solar: At the end of 2025, FPL had a total of approximately 7,932 MW1 of utility-owned solar generation, all of which are photovoltaic (PV) facilities. These solar sites are located throughout FPL’s service area. The resource plan presented in this Site Plan continues to show significant increases in solar resources over the ten-year reporting period. Approximately 12,293 MW of additional, cost-effective solar generation is projected to be added in the 2026 through 2035 time period. These solar MW consist of solar facilities that are projected to be 74.5 MW each. When combining these projected additional solar facilities with the approximate 7,932 MW of solar already installed on FPL’s system at the end of 2025, FPL’s projected total of solar capacity by the end of 2035 is 20,225 MW. FPL received cost recovery approval for the 2026 solar additions in this year’s resource plan in its settlement agreement in the 2025 rate case filing. FPL’s solar additions of 1,192 MW in 2027, 1,490 MW in 2028, and 1,788 MW in 2029 are eligible for cost recovery through the Solar and Battery Base Rate Adjustment (SoBRA) mechanism outlined in the 2025 Settlement Agreement. FPL’s resource plan also includes an additional 298 MW in 2028, 596 MW in 2029, and 745 MW in 2030 that are projected to be cost-effective resource additions. The other solar additions shown in this Site Plan in the years 2034 and 2035 are also projected to be cost-effective. FPL’s resource planning work in 2026 and beyond will continue to analyze the projected system economics of these later solar additions.
