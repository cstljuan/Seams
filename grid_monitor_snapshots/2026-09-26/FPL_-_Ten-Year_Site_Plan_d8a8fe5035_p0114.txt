III.E.4 Transmission Facilities for the Sand Pine Solar Energy Center in Calhoun County The work required to connect the approximate 74.5 MW (nameplate, AC) Sand Pine Solar Energy Center in Calhoun County in the 2nd Quarter of 2026 is projected to be: I.Substation: 1. Extend 230 kV bus at Quincy substation to a new substation (Chinkapin) and interconnect the 230/34.5kV transformer through a 230kV breaker. 2. Construct 34.5 kV bus to connect the PV array to Chinkapin 230 kV Substation. 3. Add relays and other protective equipment. 4. Breaker replacements: None. II.Transmission: 1. No additional upgrades are expected to be necessary at this time. Florida Power & Light Company 100
