Extend 230 kV bus at Ponna substation to a new Cheriot substation and interconnect the 230/34.5kV transformer through a 230kV breaker. 2. Construct 34.5 kV bus to connect the PV array to Cheriot 230 kV Substation. 3. Add relays and other protective equipment. 4. Breaker replacements: None II.Transmission: 1. No additional upgrades are expected to be necessary at this time. Florida Power & Light Company 121
