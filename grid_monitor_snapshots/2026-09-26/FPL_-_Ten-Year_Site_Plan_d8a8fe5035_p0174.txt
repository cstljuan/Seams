Substation: 1. Extend 230 kV bus at Notts substation and interconnect the 230/34.5kV transformer through a 230kV breaker. 2. Construct 34.5 kV bus to connect the PV array to Notts 230 kV Substation. 3. Add relays and other protective equipment. 4. Breaker replacements: None II. Transmission: 1.
