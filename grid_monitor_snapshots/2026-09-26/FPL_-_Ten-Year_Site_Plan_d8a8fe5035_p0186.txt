III.E.76 Transmission Facilities for the Leafcutter Solar Energy Center in DeSoto County The work required to connect the approximate 74.5 MW (nameplate, AC) Leafcutter Solar Energy Center in DeSoto County in the 1st Quarter of 2029 is projected to be: I. Substation: 1. Extend 230 kV bus at Tern substation and interconnect the 230/34.5kV transformer through a 230kV breaker. 2. Construct 34.5 kV bus to connect the PV array to Tern 230 kV Substation. 3. Add relays and other protective equipment. 4. Breaker replacements: None II. Transmission: 1. No additional transmission work is required. 2. No upgrades are expected to be necessary at this time.
