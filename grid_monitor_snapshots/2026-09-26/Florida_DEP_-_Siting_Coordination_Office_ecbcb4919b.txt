Transmission Line Project (TA26-21): For the construction, operation, and maintenance of a new transmission line corridor with 500 and 230 kV circuits. The proposed corridor is approximately 31.2 miles long beginning at the Andytown Substation in Broward County and ending at the Oasis Substation in Miami-Dade County. This application is being processed under the Florida Electric Transmission Line Siting Act, Sections 403.52-.5365 , Florida Statutes (F.S.) . DEP Siting Coordination Case Managers: Nate Senn , (850) 717-9111, and Marbelis Lugo .
