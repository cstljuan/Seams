Why You Should Go Electric Save Money Enjoy lower fuel and maintenance costs. Make a Difference Reduce your carbon footprint with a zero-emissions vehicle. Drive a Better Car Experience enhanced performance, with a quieter and smoother ride. Convenient Charging Charge at home or at more than 300 charging stations in Greater Orlando. Go Farther Than You Think Drive an average of 275 miles on one charge. Get Rebates Take advantage of rebates and incentives at the time of purchase. Types of Electric Vehicles Find an EV that fits your needs and lifestyle. Download the Buyers guide Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEV) A plug-in hybrid electric vehicle is similar to conventional hybrids but has a larger battery that can be recharged. PHEVs are usually designed with an electric-only range of 10 to 40 miles, after which the vehicle continues to operate using a gasoline engine or generator. All-Battery Electric Vehicles (BEV) An all-battery electric vehicle is powered entirely by electricity from the utility grid. BEVs currently have an average range of 275 miles.
