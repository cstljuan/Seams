Register Electric Vehicle Initiatives OUC is leading the charge by installing electric vehicle charging stations across Central Florida along with supporting the electrification of public transportation, while also transitioning its own electric vehicle fleet. OUC ReCharge Mobility Hubs Equipped with cutting-edge DC Fast chargers (DCFC), our hubs provide seamless, hassle-free charging for all types of EVs, ensuring that your vehicle is powered up and ready to go in no time. OUC is committed to building eight ReCharge Mobility Hubs in Central Florida by 2030. VISIT A HUB EV Charging Around Town OUC owns or maintains more than 300 charging stations around the region. These charging stations are part of OUC’s efforts to enhance EV infrastructure in Central Florida. FIND A CHARGER OUC’s Electric Fleet Using all-electric and hybrid vehicles, OUC is working to reduce our own carbon footprint. Electrifying the OUC fleet also lowers fuel and maintenance costs, enhancing operational efficiency and sustainability. Looking to electrify your fleet? Make the switch to cleaner, more efficient electric vehicles with OUC’s expert solutions. Electrify Your Fleet Investing in Public Transportation In partnership with LYNX, OUC installed a charging hub for the transit agency’s e-bus fleet and supports their ongoing electrification efforts. LEARN MORE Awards and Certifications OUC is an EMPOWER Program Partner and certified by EMPOWER as an EV-friendly workplace. About EMPOWER OUC’s Robinson ReCharge Mobility Hub was recognized with a Golden Brick Award of Sustainability & Resiliency from the Downtown Orlando Partnership (DOP).
