Tampa Electric Forms Report Electricity Theft What is Electricity Theft? Electricity theft occurs when someone tampers with a meter or illegally connects to the power system to avoid paying for electricity. It’s not just dishonest, it’s dangerous. Why It’s a Serious Problem Tampering with electric meters or power lines can cause fires, explosions and even electrocution. These risks endanger not only the person stealing electricity but also the public and utility workers. Even if no accident happens right away, altered equipment can remain hazardous. How to Spot Electricity Theft Be alert for signs of suspicious activity, like these. Unauthorized people working on or near electric meters Digging near underground lines or tampering with overhead wires Someone boasting about unusually low electric bills or bypassing their meter If you suspect electricity theft, report it immediately by calling 888-223-0800 or submit this form . Your report helps protect the community and keeps electricity costs fair for everyone. How We Help At Tampa Electric, we take electricity theft seriously. Our experts use advanced metering data to detect suspicious usage. Offenders may face immediate disconnection, civil penalties and even criminal charges. Call 888-223-0800 or submit this form to report suspicious activity to support reliable, fair electricity for everyone. Your vigilance makes a difference!
