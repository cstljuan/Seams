Tampa Electric Welcome Welcome to Tampa Electric Welcome, from Tampa Electric We’re glad you’re here. Whether you’re moving into a new home or growing your business, you can count on us to deliver safe, reliable energy and customer service focused on your needs. For more than a century, we’ve been proud to serve West Central Florida, helping power the moments that matter every day. This page is designed to help you get started quickly and confidently. You can set up your account, choose how you’d like to manage your service and explore programs that may help you save energy and money. You’ll also learn more about how we invest in our communities and work to build a stronger, more resilient energy future. Start, stop or transfer your service Moving? Start, stop or transfer your electric service 24/7 at your convenience. Our online tool helps you: Set up service and pick the installation date that works best for you. Receive your new account number, upload documents and get real-time confirmations as you go. Stay informed and organized with AutoPay, Paperless Billing and account notifications by text, email or voice. Save energy and lower your bill with tips, tools and programs. Get exclusive offers on TV, internet, security systems, moving services and more. Manage your energy experience Manage your energy experience with tools designed for convenience and flexibility. Register for an online account to: View bills and make payments Track your energy use Go paperless for added security and convenience Select payment options that fit your needs Manage communication preferences Understand how you use energy and find ways to save. View your Interactive Bill. Compare daily, monthly and yearly energy usage. Get personalized energy-saving recommendations. Enroll in energy-saving programs that help lower your bill. Get the information you want, when you want it. Sign up for email, text or phone notifications for bills, payments, outages and appointments. Subscribe to our residential and business newsletters for product and service announcements, energy-saving tips, community stories, special offers and other essential information. Follow us on social media. Explore programs and services Explore our programs and services to discover ways to lower your energy costs. We offer dozens of energy-saving programs designed to help homes and businesses reduce energy usage and lower electric bills. Protect what matters with Zap Cap Systems®. It helps safeguard your home or business from damaging power surges with advanced protection and backup options. Improve the safety and visibility of your business with Bright Choices® Outdoor Lighting . It offers quality, energy-efficient outdoor lighting with no upfront costs and no maintenance fees. Explore business programs, incentives and economic development resources that help power your success. Power through storms and severe weather Be prepared. Visit our Storm Center to: Learn how to stay safe around electricity at home or at work. Prepare for the possibility of a power outage Understand how power restoration works Stay informed during changing conditions We’re building a more resilient grid . We work year-round to strengthen our grid for fewer outages, faster restoration and lower repair costs from severe weather. To deliver reliable power in blue skies and gray, we’re: Trimming trees that can cause outages Undergrounding select power lines Upgrading and modernizing equipment and power poles Protecting critical infrastructure from flooding We restore power safely and efficiently. After a power outage, we follow a proven process that prioritizes safety, essential services and restoring power to the greatest number of customers as quickly and safely as possible. Care for our environment We’re committed to protecting the natural resources and wildlife that make our region unique. We invite you to explore the Florida Conservation and Technology Center , home to our award-winning Manatee Viewing Center and our Tom Hernandez Clean Energy Center. You’ll find research, habitat restoration, help for endangered species, education and fun from Tampa Electric, the Florida Aquarium and the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission. Help for neighbors in need We understand that life can be challenging at times, and we can help. We offer a variety of payment options , including flexible payment plans and our Share program to help customers facing a crisis, and we’ve partnered with organizations that offer further support and services.
